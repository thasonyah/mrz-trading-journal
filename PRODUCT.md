# Product Context

Mr.Z Trading Journal is a private trading journal for discretionary traders who need to prove whether their setups have an edge. The core workflow is logging each trade with market structure and psychology context, then reviewing analytics by setup, session, POI, rule quality, and RR behavior.

The user needs support for POI(OB), FVG, BOS, CHoCH, Liquidity Sweep, HTF/MTF/LTF, setup grades, Max RR, MFE/MAE, psychology, rule violations, account separation, and coach/admin review.

Primary success means a trader can record a trade in under a minute, review historical trades without losing chart context, and identify which setup conditions are worth repeating.
